# 🎨 Park Haven — Photorealistic 3D Park & Interactive Auth Experience

A production-quality authentication experience featuring a **frosted glassmorphic authentication panel** on the left (30%) and a **photorealistic animated 3D park scene** on the right (70%), built with React 18, Three.js, React Three Fiber, GSAP, Framer Motion, and Tailwind CSS.

---

## 🌟 Visual Realism Upgrades

### 1. 🌅 Photorealistic HDR 360 Environment & Atmospheric Lighting
- **Equirectangular 360 Sky Panoramas**: High-resolution golden hour sunburst sky dome and star-filled tranquil night sky.
- **IBL (Image-Based Lighting)**: Drei `Environment` reflections illuminating metals, water, wood, and characters with natural physical radiance.
- **ACES Filmic Tone Mapping**: Accurate color reproduction and filmic exposure (`toneMappingExposure={1.15}`).
- **Directional Sunlight & Soft Shadows**: Soft shadow mapping with bias correction and smooth falloff.

### 2. 🌿 PBR Terrain & Cobblestone Path Textures
- **Seamless Lawn Texture**: Realistic lawn grass and rich dark loam soil with repeating texture coordinates.
- **Cobblestone Flagstone Paths**: Weathered stone flagstones with delicate moss in crevices, complete with bump and roughness mapping.

### 3. 🌊 Multi-Frequency Water Physics & Caustics
- **Natural Ripple Swells & Sparkle**: Real-time water shader with multi-frequency vertex waves, caustic light patterns, golden sky fresnel reflections, and specular sun glints.
- **Botanical Details**: Floating lily pads, pink lotus blooms, granite stones, and a weathered wooden footbridge.

### 4. 🖌️ The Hero Painter & Masterpiece Canvas Engine
- **Live Oil Masterpiece Painting**: The hero painter's canvas progressively reveals a museum-grade impressionist oil painting of the sunlit park (in the style of Monet and Renoir) with visible impasto brushstrokes, linen primer grain, and pencil underdrawings!
- **Color Sync**: Dips into 3D oil paint mounds on a walnut palette, changing the brush tip color in real-time.
- **Secondary Impressionist**: Stool-seated artist painting expressive companion strokes on a separate easel.

### 5. 🎬 Cinematic Post-Processing
- **Selective Bloom**: Warm luminance glow on the golden sun and water reflections.
- **Chromatic Aberration & Vignette**: Subtle lens dispersion and framing.
- **SMAA Anti-Aliasing**: Crisp geometric edges across all hardware tiers.
